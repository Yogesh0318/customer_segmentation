# customer_segmentation
# Customer Shopping Data Analysis

## 📊 Overview
This project provides a detailed analysis of customer shopping behavior, product performance, and financial insights across multiple retail locations. The goal is to uncover actionable insights that drive **retail growth** and improve customer engagement.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights
- **Total Revenue:** $251.51M  
- **Total Customers:** 99.46K  
- **Average Spend Per Customer:** $2.53K  
- **Most Popular Category:** Clothing  
- **Dominant Payment Method:** Cash  

---

## 👥 Customer Demographics
- **Gender Split:**
  - Female: 59K customers (59.81%) → $152M revenue  
  - Male: 40K customers (40.19%) → $101M revenue  

- **Age Groups:**
  - 46–60 years → $72M revenue (most valuable segment)  
  - 26–35 years → strong growth potential  

---

## 🛍️ Product Performance
- **Top Category:** Clothing (34K transactions, 16.5K cash payments)  
- **Secondary Categories:**  
  - Cosmetics → 15K transactions  
  - Food & Beverage → 13K transactions  
  - Toys → 10K transactions  

- **Growth Opportunities:** Books, Souvenirs, Technology  

---

## 💳 Financial Insights
- **Cash Revenue:** $113M (dominant method)  
- **Credit Cards:** $88M  
- **Debit Cards:** $51M  

👉 Suggests strong reliance on cash but potential to expand digital payment adoption.  

---

## 📍 Geographical Performance
- **Top Malls:**  
  - Mall of Istanbul → $39M  
  - Kanyon → $37M  
  - Metropol AVM → $25M  

⚡ Significant performance gap after top 3 malls → opportunity to replicate success strategies.  

---

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Target Female Shoppers** → Clothing & Cosmetics campaigns.  
2. **Engage High-Value Age Groups** → Loyalty programs for 46–60, nurture 26–35.  
3. **Optimize Top Malls** → Create a "success playbook" from Istanbul & Kanyon.  
4. **Promote Digital Payments** → Incentives for credit/debit card usage.
